Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

"Channel tools" is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels I am trapped, Scientific Disasters and Red Flag Marriage to upload and publish videos to their own channels through the YouTube Data API.

What data it uses

  • It signs in only to the owner's own Google account, with the owner's permission, to upload videos and update their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and comments.
  • It does not collect, store or share any data about anyone else.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never sent to anyone else.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Through the contact options on the channels' YouTube pages.
